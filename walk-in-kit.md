## Who to walk into
Per the roadmap: a café, sandwich shop, food stall, or bakery. Independent, not a chain (chains need head-office sign-off, dead end for a walk-in). Look for: a counter, a queue at peak times, a phone that rings mid-service.

## The 90-second pitch
Open with a question, not a pitch — let them say the problem out loud themselves:

> "Hi — quick one, do you ever get a queue building up, or the phone ringing while you're mid-order?"

*(they'll say yes — every counter business has this)*

> "I built something for exactly that. Customers scan a QR code on your counter, order and pay from their phone, it lands straight on a screen here — no app to download, and zero commission, you keep 100% of every order for a flat £12 a month. I've got it running live on a real café right now — want to see it on your counter?"

## The demo (2 minutes, on their counter)
1. Open `ordernook.uk/corner-grind` on your phone.
2. Tap an item (Latte or Americano) — show the size/milk/extras options: *"this is exactly what your menu looks like, with your name and colours instead."*
3. Add to cart, open the cart — point at **"Pay when I collect"** vs **"Pay online now"**: *"zero commission either way — money goes straight to your own Stripe account, never through me."*
4. Switch tabs to the dashboard, logged in as corner-grind — show a live order landing with Accept/Reject/Ready buttons.
5. Show the QR poster screenshot: *"this goes on your counter — that's the entire setup."*

## Objection handling
- **"I already give customers my number."** → "Sure, but that means you stopping what you're doing to answer it mid-rush. This takes orders even when you're slammed."
- **"Sounds like another subscription."** → "It replaces the phone-order chaos, and there's no per-order cut like Uber Eats or Deliveroo — £12 pays for itself with under 2 orders a month you'd otherwise have missed."
- **"Will my customers actually use it?"** → "Same motion as scanning a menu QR code, which most people already do. 30-day free trial, no card — if nobody uses it, you've lost nothing."
- **"What if I get swamped with orders?"** → "You're in control — there's a pause/sold-out toggle so you never get more than you can handle."

## The close — sign them up on the spot
> "Want to try it free for 30 days? No card needed, two minutes right now."

1. Go to `ordernook.uk/dashboard/register` on your phone or laptop.
2. They enter: business name, email, password.
3. Offer to build their first 5 menu items together right there — name + price is enough to go live.
4. Generate their QR code (`dashboard/qr` → download PNG or open printable poster).
5. *"Print it, put it on the counter — you're live today."*

## If they say "let me think about it"
Don't push. Leave them with:
- `ordernook.uk` — they can register any time
- `support@zizweit.uk`
- "No pressure — it's free for 30 days whenever you want to try it."
